LIFESAVING KNOWLEDGE

Emergency Preparedness & Action Guide

Quick, reliable steps to perform while waiting for professional emergency medical or police services.

Adult CPR & Severe Medical Response

If someone is unresponsive and not breathing normally:

  1. Check Responsiveness: Gently tap shoulders and shout "Are you okay?". Check if breathing.
  2. Call 112 / 102 Immediately: Put on speakerphone while starting care.
  3. Hand-Only CPR: Place hands in the center of the chest. Push hard and fast at 100–120 beats per minute (e.g. to the tempo of "Stayin' Alive").
  4. Allow Full Chest Recoil: Compress down at least 2 inches (5 cm) each beat without leaning on chest between compressions.

⚠️ Heart Attack Signs

  • Chest pain, pressure, fullness or squeezing
  • Pain radiating to jaw, left arm, neck, or back
  • Shortness of breath, dizziness, cold sweat
  • Action: Keep patient calm and seated. Do not let them walk. Call 102/112 immediately.

Road Collision & Trauma Response

Prioritize scene safety and prevent secondary collisions:

  1. Secure the Scene: Turn on vehicle hazard lights. Place reflective warning triangles at least 30 meters back.
  2. Do Not Move Victims: Never move a victim with potential neck/spine injuries unless there is an immediate fire or drowning risk.
  3. Control Heavy Bleeding: Apply firm, direct pressure over open wounds with a clean cloth. Do not remove penetrating objects.
  4. Good Samaritan Protection: Under Indian law (Supreme Court Guidelines), Good Samaritans helping accident victims are exempt from civil and criminal liability and cannot be harassed.

📞 Critical Numbers

  • 112: Unified Emergency Police & Rescue
  • 1073: National Highway Accident Helpline
  • 102 / 108: Emergency Ambulance Dispatch

Personal Safety & Harassment Protocol

When feeling unsafe, being followed, or facing immediate threat:

  1. Trust Your Instincts: Head towards public, well-lit spaces, shops, metro stations, or security booths.
  2. Trigger Silent SOS / Broadcast: Share your live location link with your trusted contacts network immediately.
  3. Make Noise if Necessary: Use the Audio Beacon Alarm or shout "Fire!" or "Help!" to draw immediate attention.
  4. Call 1091 / 112: Women Helpline 1091 provides dedicated rapid police intervention.

🛡️ Digital Safety Habit

  • Keep trusted contacts updated before traveling alone late at night.
  • Keep phone location services enabled in advance.
  • Keep phone battery charged above 25% when in transit.

Fire, Smoke & Chemical Hazard Protocol

In the event of a residential or workplace fire outbreak:

  1. Crawl Low in Smoke: Smoke and toxic fumes rise. Stay near the floor where air is cleaner.
  2. Feel Doors Before Opening: Use the back of your hand to feel the doorknob. If hot, do not open—find an alternate exit.
  3. Never Use Elevators: Always evacuate using fire exit stairs.
  4. Call 101 / 112: Inform the Fire Brigade of your exact floor and building address immediately.

🧯 Fire Extinguisher (P.A.S.S.)

  • Pull the safety pin.
  • Aim at the base of the fire.
  • Squeeze the handle lever.
  • Sweep from side to side.

📱 Real Android Background Execution: Technical Realities & Restrictions ▼
✅ What Works on Android:
  • Native Foreground Service: With FOREGROUND_SERVICE_TYPE_MICROPHONE, Android OS allows microphone capture even when the app is placed in the background or minimized.
  • Screen Locked / Screen Off: Using a PARTIAL_WAKE_LOCK, the CPU continues running offline keyword spotting while the display is locked.
  • Auto-Reboot Recovery: BootCompletedReceiver automatically relaunches the listening service after phone reboot if the user opted in.
⚠️ Hard Operating System Restrictions:
  • Force-Stop: If a user force-stops the app via System Settings, Android terminates all background services and alarms until manual reopen.
  • OEM Aggressive Battery Killers: Manufacturers (Xiaomi/MIUI, OnePlus/OxygenOS, Samsung OneUI) frequently kill background processes unless exempted under "Battery Optimization → Don't Optimize / Unrestricted" and "Autostart".
  • Android 14+ Microphone Indicator: Android displays an orange/green microphone privacy indicator in the status bar while the service is listening to ensure user consent.
  • Pure Web Browsers: Mobile browsers (Chrome/Safari) suspend Web Audio and Web Speech when the screen locks or tabs switch to protect battery and privacy. For screen-off detection, the native Android APK build is required.
